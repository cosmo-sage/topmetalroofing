# TopMetalRoofing — Design System

Dark industrial, engineering-grade spec-sheet aesthetic. Every competitor in residential
roofing runs light and suburban-friendly; we run dark, confident, and technical.
Principles distilled from high-performing roofing sites (Erie Home, Janney, Roof Squad,
Eaton) — original expression, no copied assets.

## Brand Voice

- Direct, confident, spec-driven. Numbers beat adjectives.
- Lead with durability facts: wind ratings (up to 120 mph), hail resistance, lifetime
  transferable warranty, gauge steel, coating systems.
- Short declarative headlines. No hype words ("amazing", "incredible"). No exclamation marks.
- Trust must be quantified: review counts, years, installs completed, warranty terms.

## Layout Principles

1. **Hero = one screen, one action.** Bold condensed headline, 2–3 line spec claim
   (wind/hail/warranty), single primary CTA "Get My Free Quote" + click-to-call phone
   number visible without scrolling.
2. **Proof strip directly under hero** — review count, rating, warranty badge, years in
   business. Always visible on every page.
3. **Spec-sheet sections.** Durability claims presented as spec tables/stat blocks, not
   paragraph prose. Large numbers, small labels.
4. **3-step process graphic** (Consultation → Material Selection → Installation) to make
   the decision feel managed. Each step: number, short title, one sentence.
5. **Financing is a first-class section** — headline offer (e.g. $0 down), 2 sentences,
   CTA. Not a footnote.
6. **Project gallery** — filterable grid (standing seam, metal shingle, agricultural,
   commercial). Real project photos, location captions.
7. **Local anchoring** — service-area list in footer, city names in page titles/H2s.
8. **Click-to-call repeated**: hero, mid-page after gallery, footer. Phone number as
   large tappable text on mobile.

## Spacing & Rhythm

- Section padding: 96px desktop / 56px mobile.
- Max content width: 1200px; text measure 65–75ch.
- Generous whitespace on dark — never dense. Panels separate content blocks.

## Typography

- Display: **Barlow Condensed** (700, uppercase for section eyebrows), tight leading (1.05).
- Body: **Inter** (400/600), 16–18px, line-height 1.6, color secondary on dark bg.
- Stat numbers: Barlow Condensed 700 at 48–72px.

## Imagery

- Real install/job-site photography over stock. Slightly desaturated, high contrast,
  metal texture close-ups welcome. Dark overlay on hero imagery (60–70% black gradient).

## Accessibility

- All text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on its background (tokens below are pre-checked).
- Focus states visible: 2px accent-orange outline.
- CTA buttons min 48px tap target.
